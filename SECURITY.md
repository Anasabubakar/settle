Do not open public issues for vulnerabilities, secrets, payment-flow flaws, document privacy concerns, or provider-destination attacks.

Email **anasabubakar7000@gmail.com** with reproduction steps, impact, and mitigation ideas. Do not include real keys or personal bill documents.
